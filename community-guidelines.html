Community Guidelines

Last updated: 16 November 2026

DriveNut brings drivers together to share experiences, ask questions and learn from one another. Keep the community helpful and safe.

Be respectful

  • Discuss ideas without insulting, bullying or targeting people.
  • Do not threaten, harass, discriminate against or impersonate anyone.
  • Respect different levels of driving experience and different backgrounds.

Put road safety first

  • Never encourage speeding, reckless driving, distracted driving or evading road rules.
  • Do not post dangerous challenges or encourage people to try risky maneuvers.
  • Share safety information responsibly and distinguish personal experience from verified guidance.
  • Never post while driving. Stop in a safe, legal place before using the app.

Protect privacy

  • Do not post passwords, phone numbers, home addresses, identity documents, live location details or private information about another person.
  • Get permission before sharing identifiable photos of other people.
  • Remember that uploaded profile and post images use public URLs.

Keep content useful and lawful

  • No spam, scams, deceptive links or repeated promotional posts.
  • Only share content you own or have permission to use.
  • Do not publish unlawful material or content that promotes harm.

Report concerns

Use the report control on a post when you believe it breaks these guidelines. Reports are reviewed by an administrator when available. Do not misuse reports to target someone because you disagree with them.

Moderation

DriveNut may review reports and take action on content that violates these guidelines. Current tools are limited: report review does not yet guarantee post removal, account suspension or an appeals workflow.

If there is an immediate threat to someone's safety, contact the appropriate local emergency service. Do not rely on community moderation for emergencies.
