Terms of Use

Last updated: 16 November 2026

These basic terms describe expected use of DriveNut, a social community for drivers. They are a starting point for product rules and have not been reviewed by a lawyer. The service operator should obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review before public launch.

Eligibility and accounts

  • You must be legally permitted to use the service where you live and comply with the age requirements of applicable law and service providers.
  • Use an email address you control and keep your sign-in credentials private.
  • Provide a display name and profile information that you are comfortable sharing with the community.
  • You are responsible for activity performed through your account. Tell the operator if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

Community conduct

Be respectful. Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, share private information about others, post unlawful content, or encourage dangerous driving. Do not use DriveNut to coordinate illegal activity or to publish instructions that put road users at risk. Follow the Community Guidelines.

Your content

You retain rights you have in content you create. By submitting content, you allow DriveNut to host, store, display and distribute it as needed to operate the community. Only upload content you have permission to share. Do not post another person's private information or images without appropriate permission.

Public visibility

Profiles, posts and uploaded images may be visible to other members or anyone with a public image URL. Do not upload confidential or sensitive information. Removing content from the website may not immediately remove copies, cached versions or backups.

Safety reports and moderation

Members may report posts. The current moderation tools allow an administrator to review a report and update its status. They do not yet provide a complete appeals process, account suspension system or guarantee of immediate review. If you see an immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service rather than relying on an in-app report.

Availability and changes

DriveNut is provided as an evolving service. Features may change, be unavailable or be removed. The operator may restrict content or access when reasonably necessary for safety, security or service operation.

Account closure

A complete self-service account deletion flow is not available in this version. Signing out does not delete an account. The operator must publish a verified support channel and a clear deletion process before inviting the public to rely on account deletion rights through this website.

Disclaimer

Community posts reflect their authors' views, not verified professional advice. Road, vehicle and safety conditions vary. Use your own judgment and follow applicable laws and official road-safety guidance.

Updates

These terms may be revised as the service changes. Continued use after an updated version is published may be subject to the revised terms, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

These terms are a product draft, not a legal guarantee. Obtain legal review and publish operator identity and contact details before public launch.
