DRIVENUT · PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 16 November 2026

DriveNut is a social community for drivers. This notice explains what information the current website handles and how community content is displayed. It is a plain-language product notice, not legal advice or a substitute for a jurisdiction-specific privacy review.

Information handled

  • Account information: your email address is handled by Supabase Authentication. DriveNut also stores your display name and driver type.
  • Profile and community content: profile biography and photo, posts, comments, likes, follows, saved-post records, community memberships, reports and notifications.
  • Technical information: the hosting and authentication providers may process routine connection, security and diagnostic information under their own terms.

How information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, profiles, the feed, discussions, notifications, community features, safety reporting and basic service security. DriveNut does not need your private address, identity documents, passwords in a post, or other sensitive personal details to operate.

What other members can see

DriveNut is a community product. Your display name, driver type, biography, profile photo and posts may be visible to signed-in members. Profile and post images are stored in a public Supabase Storage bucket, so anyone who obtains an image URL may be able to view it. Do not upload private or sensitive images or information.

Service providers

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Supabase for authentication, database and image storage. Those providers process information to deliver their services and are subject to their own privacy and security terms.

Security and retention

DriveNut uses database access policies intended to restrict account-specific actions. No internet service can promise perfect security. Information is retained while needed to operate the service and may remain in backups or provider systems according to their retention processes.

Your choices and account deletion

You can edit your profile and remove your own posts using the available controls. A complete self-service account deletion flow is not yet available in this version. Do not assume that signing out deletes your account or its data. Account-deletion requests will need to be handled by the service operator through a verified support process once that channel is published.

Children and young people

Do not use DriveNut unless you are legally allowed to use the service in your location. If you are under the age required by local law or by a provider's terms, do not create an account without any required parent or guardian involvement.

Changes

This notice may change as DriveNut features and operating arrangements evolve. The date above indicates the latest stated revision.

Before public launch, the operator should add a real privacy contact, confirm applicable legal requirements, and review provider settings and data-retention practices.
